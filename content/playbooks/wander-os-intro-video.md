Hi, I'm Sahil Mansuri, president and COO of Wander, and I'm here to introduce you to WanderOS. WanderOS is our technology platform that we've built for property managers who want to increase their direct bookings, have a seamless checkout experience for their guests, and be able to drive repeat bookings and guest loyalty.

Consumer behavior is changing entirely. Consumers only want to transact and interact on websites where they feel like they can trust the platform. Legacy direct booking websites — the property pages aren't well illustrated, and ultimately it leads to a loss of confidence. People just go back to Airbnb, which they're comfortable with. That's a huge problem because you're often one bad review away from being shadowbanned, don't have a way to drive repeat bookings, and ultimately don't have control over your own business.

Consumer habits are changing. 37% of all searches today begin on ChatGPT or other LLMs. LLMs are incentivized to send traffic to the cheapest booking option, and direct booking sites are always going to be cheaper than OTAs because OTAs take a huge chunk of revenue in fees per booking. That new paradigm means that property managers, for the first time in 20 years, have an opportunity to have their listing show up above the OTAs — and WanderOS is the only platform that's built from the ground up to be AI-search friendly, that allows your properties to show up first on ChatGPT, other LLMs, and even Google search.

When we founded Wander 4 years ago, we had to deal with the same challenges that property managers deal with today. We got 100% of our bookings and 100% of our traffic through Airbnb, and over the course of the last 4 years, we've gotten to 70% direct bookings and had over $70 million of bookings on wander.com. We've taken 4 years of knowledge of learning every trick in the book on how to break free of the OTAs and turned it into WanderOS.

WanderOS connects directly with your PMS. You'll have a brand new beautiful website in 48 hours, and you have to do none of the work in order to actually build it. Our team handles all of the onboarding and all of the design elements, with AI search tools and functionality that ensures that every guest finds the right home for them. All you need to do is sit back and watch the direct bookings come in.

The next step is the payments checkout experience. WanderOS provides secure, trusted infrastructure that has led to over $70 million of bookings on wander.com. The final step, of course, is getting them to do it again and again and again. That's where WanderOS has built an entire suite of products designed to help the guest come back year after year — whether that's phone agents that allow you to be responsive 24/7, or email agents that allow you to connect personally with every guest, which is what WanderOS powers.

We understand that property managers only make money when a guest checks out. So high monthly fees or upfront costs are a non-starter. We've made WanderOS completely free for you to get started, only charging a 3% booking fee to the guest. So nothing comes out of your pocket.

As a property manager, there are two things that are really important to you. You want to make sure that you're driving direct bookings in order to maximize the amount of revenue for your owners and for your business. And secondly, you want to make sure that you're driving guest loyalty and giving an exceptional experience to everyone who stays at one of your homes. WanderOS is your technology partner to serve both those ends.

At the end of the day, we're doing this because we love travel. We love people. We love connecting people to the world. And WanderOS is your technology platform that's built from the ground up to ensure that your business can grow and scale in the future.

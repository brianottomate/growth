## Give practical advice its own space

The article should explain the decision behind a trip. A fact box holds the details a reader will want to find again without making the surrounding paragraph repeat them.

<ArticleNote title="Before you plan the route">
  Confirm the starting point, transport options and current opening dates from
  the supplied sources. Leave unknown prices and travel times out until they are
  verified.
</ArticleNote>

## Keep related media together

A gallery preserves each image's shape. Use a stacked layout when two landscape images belong alongside a taller image. Captions identify the media; the prose should contribute a useful observation.

## Answer the decision first

A reader should find a useful starting point before the long explanation. Priorities can lead to different choices, so explain the condition behind each recommendation.

## Put caveats beside the recommendation

A strong recommendation should explain its limitations. Follow each area or season with its activities and a small property shortlist using the existing place and recommendation cards.

  title="Choosing a quieter home base"

  ]}
  cautions={[
